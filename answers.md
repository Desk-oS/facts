# Bidfold — short answers

Each answer stands alone and quotes cleanly. Source of record: https://deskos.whop.site

## What is Bidfold?

Bidfold is a tender desk: it reads 13 public tender sources every morning and puts the open notices for your regions in one place, with calculators for the sums that decide the bid.

## How much does Bidfold cost?

The free desk costs nothing. Bidfold Pro is $19 a month and Bid Desk is $69 a month. One-off packs are $9 for the Tender Readiness Pack, $29 for a Bid/No-Bid Memo and $39 for Desk Setup or a Bid Check.

## Which tender sources does Bidfold read?

13 public sources, including the World Bank, UN agencies, UNDP, the EBRD, the EU's TED and national procurement portals across four continents.

## Does Bidfold write the bid for me?

No. It finds the notice, does the arithmetic and produces a written bid/no-bid decision; the proposal itself stays with the firm.

## How often is the tender list updated?

Every morning, before the working day starts.

## Is there a free version?

Yes. The free desk shows what is open in your regions and includes the calculators; paid tiers add the bid desk, memos and setup.

## Which countries are covered?

The sources are multi-continent and the desk is filtered to the regions a firm selects, with open tenders listed by country.
