# Bidfold: tender discovery and bid tooling, in plain terms

Bidfold is a tender intelligence desk for small firms: it gathers open tenders from 13 public sources every morning and pairs them with calculators for landed cost, margin and bill-of-quantities stress.

It is built and run by Jacaranda Labs in Harare, Zimbabwe. Live: https://deskos.whop.site

## The short answer

Bidfold is a tender desk: it reads 13 public tender sources every morning and puts the open notices for your regions in one place, with calculators for the sums that decide the bid.

## Who it is for

Small and mid-sized firms bidding for public contracts, plus bid consultants.

Markets: Africa, Multi-continent public procurement.

## How it works

**Sources.** 13 public tender sources including the World Bank, UN agencies, UNDP, EBRD, the EU's TED and national portals on four continents.

**Calculators.** 30 working calculators for tender sums, each showing its formula.

**Alerts.** One email when something that fits arrives.

**Bid / No-Bid Memo.** A written decision on whether a notice is worth the week.

**Desk Setup.** The desk configured for a firm's sectors and regions.

**Readiness.** Checklists and packs for the week the right notice appears.

## What you get

- Open tenders for your regions in one place each morning
- Landed cost and margin before the bid goes in
- A written bid/no-bid decision
- Fewer deadlines discovered the week they close

## Questions people ask

### What is Bidfold?

Bidfold is a tender desk: it reads 13 public tender sources every morning and puts the open notices for your regions in one place, with calculators for the sums that decide the bid.

### How much does Bidfold cost?

The free desk costs nothing. Bidfold Pro is $19 a month and Bid Desk is $69 a month. One-off packs are $9 for the Tender Readiness Pack, $29 for a Bid/No-Bid Memo and $39 for Desk Setup or a Bid Check.

### Which tender sources does Bidfold read?

13 public sources, including the World Bank, UN agencies, UNDP, the EBRD, the EU's TED and national procurement portals across four continents.

### Does Bidfold write the bid for me?

No. It finds the notice, does the arithmetic and produces a written bid/no-bid decision; the proposal itself stays with the firm.

### How often is the tender list updated?

Every morning, before the working day starts.

### Is there a free version?

Yes. The free desk shows what is open in your regions and includes the calculators; paid tiers add the bid desk, memos and setup.

### Which countries are covered?

The sources are multi-continent and the desk is filtered to the regions a firm selects, with open tenders listed by country.
